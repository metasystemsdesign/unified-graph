# Unified Graph: Invariant Visual Semantics

Official specification for the **Invariant Visual Semantics** engineering standard, mapping the geometry of **Intellectual Capital Units (ICU)**.

---

### 📄 Introduction

Creative Information Theory ([March 2026, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19394773](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19394773)) finds its practical realization within this document.

Integration of ICU closes the gap in the provenance of the work, ensuring evidentiary continuity between the source and any of its embodiments (including creative synthesis environments). This preserves the authenticity of authorship when scaling the original work in cross-media environments (in games, cinema, the 3D industry, interactive and virtual worlds).

This standard under development is aimed at preserving cognitive energy, ensuring the privacy of the creative process, and securing the exclusive right of ownership over the results of intellectual activity.
